# Commercial Operator Runbook

Date: 2026-05-07

Purpose: define how the operator should handle SaaS sales leads generated by the Pilates landing, web consultor and WhatsApp before the real SaaS/billing/onboarding specs are finalized.

## Operating Principle

The consultor sells by diagnosis, proof and risk reduction. The goal is to guide the buyer toward the strongest fitting plan, normally 7 Agentes for broad operational needs, without pretending that a click is an active subscription.

## Daily Routine

1. Open Sales Inbox.
2. Filter `priority=hot`.
3. Handle `human_whatsapp_assist`, `checkout_intent`, `custom_agent_follow_up` and `plan_recommendation` first.
4. Check Sales Inbox filters for `priority=hot`, `waitlist_intent`, `checkout_intent` and `custom_agent_follow_up`.
5. Review optional n8n urgent alerts and digest, if configured.
6. Mark every handled lead with the next action.
7. End the day by marking stale leads as follow-up, waiting customer, lost or do-not-contact.

## Lead Priority Handling

### Hot

Signals:

- asks to subscribe;
- asks for checkout/payment;
- asks for human before closing;
- asks about the recommended plan after diagnosis;
- gives contact and has broad/multi-agent pain;
- asks for Agente sob medida.
- submits or clicks through a custom-agent diagnostic report.

Operator action:

- take over when needed;
- answer final objection;
- send plan page or checkout link only when gated;
- mark `checkout_sent`, `waiting_customer`, `follow_up_scheduled`, `won` or `lost`.

### Warm

Signals:

- asks about price/plans;
- asks how the product works;
- describes pain but has not decided;
- wants WhatsApp continuation.

Operator action:

- keep AI active unless they ask for human;
- use consultor/WhatsApp follow-up;
- route to `/pilates/planos` after context;
- schedule follow-up only with consent/contact.

### Cold

Signals:

- asks vague questions;
- no contact;
- no clear pain;
- just explores.

Operator action:

- let AI continue;
- do not send checkout;
- do not create aggressive follow-up;
- ask one helpful diagnostic question.

## Sales Inbox Actions

| Action | Use when | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `take_over` | Lead asks for human or operator wants to assume | Pauses WhatsApp AI replies |
| `send_whatsapp_message` | Operator replies to a WhatsApp lead | Uses server-side Meta adapter |
| `send_plan_page` | Lead explicitly wants to compare plans | Sends trusted `/pilates/planos` |
| `send_checkout_link` | Lead confirmed buying intent or plan | Does not mark paid/subscribed |
| `schedule_follow_up` | Lead gave contact/permission and needs later contact | Do not schedule if opted out |
| `resume_ai` | Human conversation can return to AI | Use only when safe |
| `mark_waiting_customer` | Operator replied and waits | Keep next action clear |
| `mark_won` | Commercially won/closed in pipeline | Not billing confirmation |
| `mark_lost` | Lead will not move forward | Stop active selling |
| `mark_do_not_contact` | Opt-out or should not be contacted | Terminal for automation |
| `edit_lead_summary` | Summary needs correction | Keep it safe, no raw sensitive details |

## When To Send Plans

Send `/pilates/planos` when:

- buyer asks to see/compare plans;
- consultor has enough context to recommend a plan;
- buyer wants price after receiving a short consultative answer;
- buyer has completed/progressed through the future real demo;
- buyer is choosing between Base, 1, 3 or 7 Agentes.

Do not send plans as the first default move from a cold landing CTA.

## When To Send Checkout

Send checkout only when:

- buyer explicitly says they want to sign/subscribe/pay;
- buyer confirms the recommended plan;
- buyer clicks checkout on `/pilates/planos`;
- operator-assisted close is happening from Sales Inbox.

Before sending checkout, make sure the buyer can access answers for:

- 30-day guarantee;
- setup in a few minutes after confirmed payment;
- studio-owned WhatsApp;
- hard message caps;
- human control;
- payment data safety.

## How To Handle Common Objections

| Objection | Operator stance |
| --- | --- |
| "Achei caro" | Reframe around missed students, manual reception time, lost renewals and complete-system value. Lower plans are narrower starts, not equal defaults. |
| "Quero testar gratis" | No public trial. Explain 30-day guarantee when checkout terms are available. |
| "Tenho medo de configurar errado" | Explain self-guided setup with consultor/agent help and simple initial configuration. |
| "Meu WhatsApp vai ser usado?" | Paying studios use their own connected WhatsApp for their agents. The SaaS sales consultor uses our own WhatsApp. |
| "E se passar da cota?" | Hard cap. Upgrade or buy extra quota; no surprise over-limit usage. |
| "Quero marketing" | Agente sob medida. Ask what operation they want, collect contact and say the team will follow up. |
| Diagnostic says `mapped_solution` | Treat as normal SaaS sales. Use mapped agents and diagnostic context to move toward consultor/demo/plans, not custom proposal. |
| Diagnostic says `custom_agent` | Treat as custom-agent proposal lead. Ask scope questions first, then collect contact and schedule follow-up. |
| Diagnostic says `mixed_solution` | Sell the mapped SaaS path and keep the custom operation as a separate proposal/upsell. |
| Diagnostic says `unclear` | Ask for missing details before recommending SaaS plan or custom proposal. |
| "Quero humano" | Route/take over in WhatsApp and keep safe summary. |
| "Pode pegar meus dados de pagamento aqui?" | No. Payment details only in secure provider checkout. |

## WhatsApp Rules

- AI can answer inbound/opted-in messages.
- Human takeover pauses AI.
- External spreadsheets/CRMs are not a reply surface.
- Operator replies must go through Sales Inbox or an approved shared inbox/provider surface.
- Out-of-window proactive follow-up requires approved WhatsApp templates.
- Opt-out stops automated replies and proactive follow-up.

## Sales Inbox/n8n Rules

- Sales Inbox/Postgres is the v1 pipeline/reporting source of truth.
- Sales Inbox is the real-time control surface.
- n8n alerts are secondary, not the lead database.
- If n8n fails, continue operating in Sales Inbox and retry later.
- External CRM/spreadsheet sync is disabled for this phase.

## What Not To Do

- Do not promise a fake demo.
- Do not route cold visitors directly to checkout.
- Do not mark a lead as paid/subscribed from chat, Sales Inbox, external CRM/spreadsheet or checkout return.
- Do not collect card data, CVV, passwords or payment credentials.
- Do not auto-merge leads using only studio name or similar conversation.
- Do not include Agente sob medida in public plans.
- Do not offer public discounts unless trusted operator config later allows it.
- Do not confuse this Sales Inbox with the future paying-studio inbox.

## End States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ai_active` | AI continues the conversation |
| `handoff_requested` | Human/operator attention requested |
| `human_active` | Operator owns the conversation; AI paused |
| `waiting_customer` | Operator replied and waits |
| `follow_up_scheduled` | Follow-up planned with permission |
| `checkout_sent` | Trusted checkout link sent; not paid |
| `won` | Commercially won in pipeline; billing still owns payment truth |
| `lost` | Lead will not continue |
| `do_not_contact` | Stop contact/automation |

## Minimal Daily Metrics

Track manually or via n8n until the real SaaS analytics exists:

- new leads by channel;
- hot leads;
- human handoffs;
- plan page sends;
- checkout links sent;
- won/lost;
- custom-agent requests;
- n8n automation failures;
- WhatsApp send failures;
- top objections.
